Shyam Pathak

Founder and teacher, SM Tutorials

I started SM Tutorials in 2021 with a bench, a blackboard and four children from Koyna Colony. My younger cousin Aarti was failing maths in Std 8, and sitting with her every evening showed me the problem was never her brain. She was simply too afraid to ask.

That is why I cap every batch at 20. A child who is afraid of a big class will open up in a small one. Parents here work hard and expect honest answers, so I tell them exactly where their child stands, every month.
